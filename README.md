## 🚨 𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗥𝗘𝗦𝗤𝗡𝗘𝗧?

**RESQNET** is an **AI-powered disaster response and management platform** designed to provide useful information, awareness, and assistance during emergency situations.

🌪️ Natural disasters can happen without warning.

🌧️ Weather conditions can change rapidly.

📍 Location can make a huge difference.

🤖 **RESQNET brings AI, real-time weather data, geographic information, and emergency resources together in one platform.**
